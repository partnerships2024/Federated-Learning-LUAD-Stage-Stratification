Research Context & Privacy

The prediction is generated by a research-stage federated learning model trained on retrospective LUAD genomic and clinical data.

Raw genomic records remain local to the research client.

The current model consumes mutation-centred 301-bp sequence windows. Raw Oxford Nanopore MinION FASTQ integration is planned as future work.

Patient identity and research reports remain within this local research client. No patient name, raw genomic sequence, or report content is sent to the central federated server.
